Help with the app, your purchase and your account

Contact us

Something not working, or a question we don't answer below? Email hrajput4@gmail.com. It helps to include your device, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of the Account tab).

Free plays

Every game can be played free a few times each day, and your free plays come back the next day. Practice boards don't use them, and when you run out you can watch an optional ad for an extra play. Pro Lifetime makes plays unlimited in every game.

Restoring Pro Lifetime

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase that stays with your App Store account. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Signing in with Apple in the Account tab is optional; it also brings your hints back on another device.

Hints

You start with 10 free hints. You can earn more by watching an optional rewarded ad, and with Pro Lifetime hints are free.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the Account tab and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account and your hint balance, and stops the app using your Apple ID. Pro Lifetime stays with your App Store account, and Restore Purchases brings it back.
