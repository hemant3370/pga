Privacy Policy

Puzzle Games Arena

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

You can play Puzzle Games Arena without an account or any personal details: the app gives you an anonymous player ID so your hints and purchases keep working. Signing in with Apple is optional; if you do, we keep the name and email Apple shares with us so your Pro purchase and hints follow you to other devices. Free players see ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for hints, an extra play or more time. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

DataWhyLinked to you?
Player ID Created automatically on first launch (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). It keys your hint balance, your purchase record and crash reports. Yes. On its own it doesn't reveal who you are.

We don't collect your physical address, phone number, photos, contacts or location, and we don't sell your data.

Third-party services we use

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in (anonymous and Sign in with Apple), the server-side hint balance, app settings delivered remotely, and Crashlytics crash reports.
  • Sign in with Apple — optional sign-in; Apple decides what it shares, and you can hide your email.
  • RevenueCat — validates Pro Lifetime purchases made through the App Store.
  • Google AdMob — banner ads and rewarded ads for free players.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies: Firebase, Apple, RevenueCat, and Google.

Your choices

  • Sign in with Apple is optional. You can play, buy Pro and use hints without it. You can sign out anytime in the app's Account tab.
  • App Tracking Transparency. We ask for tracking permission before the first ad is requested, not at launch. You can change your answer anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Pro removes ads. Pro players don’t see banner or rewarded ads.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple. In Settings › your name › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple, choose Puzzle Games Arena › Stop Using. The app then signs you out.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in with Apple: open the Account tab and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account, your name and email, and your hint balance and reward records from our server, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. It doesn't refund or remove a Pro Lifetime purchase, which stays with your Apple ID; manage purchases in your App Store account settings.
  • Not signed in: uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. To have your anonymous hint balance deleted from our server too, email us (below).

Children

Puzzle Games Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app doesn't require an account or personal details to play.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
